Who We Are

More Than an Organisation —
A Movement

Brains Beyond Measure NPC is a community-rooted non-profit company based in Mapetla, Soweto. Founded by Dineo Moletsane, we exist to uplift the young people and vulnerable members of our community through meaningful programmes that address education, wellness, food security, and creative expression.

We are formally registered with the CIPC (Reg No: 2025/553907/08) and officially endorsed by the Ward 16 Councillor of the City of Johannesburg. Our work is driven by a simple truth: every brain holds potential beyond measure.

"Brains Beyond Measure remains committed to empowering young people through educational support, motivation, wellness initiatives, sports, arts, and community programmes."

Dineo Moletsane

Founder & Director

Driven by a passion for youth mentorship, public speaking, and the arts, Dineo has dedicated her career to building programmes that create real change in the communities she serves. Her guiding belief: "Art is Love Defined."

What We Do

Our Programmes

Every programme we run is designed to address a real, pressing need in our community. From feeding hungry children to equipping learners for life beyond school, we show up where it matters most.

🍲

Mapetla Soup Kitchen

Now in its second year, the Mapetla Soup Kitchen provides daily nutritious meals to children and elderly residents in Mapetla who depend on this support. More than food — we offer dignity, care, hope, and unity.

  • Daily meals for children & the elderly
  • 1-year anniversary milestone in May 2026
  • Endorsed by Ward 16 Councillor, City of Johannesburg
  • A cornerstone of community support
🎤

Youth Motivational & Wellness Visits

We visit high schools to empower learners with the mindset, tools, and motivation they need to thrive — academically and personally. Led by Themba Lukhele in partnership with the Skeem GP Foundation.

  • Motivational speaking sessions
  • Mental wellness discussions
  • Exam preparation & time management
  • Goal-setting & leadership development
  • Peer pressure & social challenges guidance
🎨

Arts & Culture

We believe that creativity is a pathway to confidence, healing, and identity. Our arts programming gives young people a voice and a platform to express themselves and discover their gifts.

  • Creative arts workshops
  • Self-expression & confidence building
  • Cultural identity & community pride
⚽

Sports Development

Sport teaches discipline, teamwork, and resilience. We incorporate sports development into our youth empowerment model to build well-rounded young leaders in Mapetla and beyond.

  • Youth sports initiatives
  • Team building & discipline
  • Physical health & wellbeing

Our Story

Making a Difference

2017

The Journey Begins

Dineo Moletsane begins building Brains Beyond Measure, working at the grassroots level to serve the youth and community of Mapetla, Soweto.

May 2025

Mapetla Soup Kitchen Launched

The Soup Kitchen opens its doors, beginning daily operations feeding children and elderly residents who depend on this lifeline in Ward 16.

11 July 2025

Formally Registered as NPC

Brains Beyond Measure is registered with CIPC as a Non-Profit Company (Reg No: 2025/553907/08), formalising its commitment to the community.

May 2026

Soup Kitchen Turns One

The Mapetla Soup Kitchen celebrates its 1-year anniversary — a milestone of consistency, community love, and impact in feeding hundreds of vulnerable residents.

Ongoing

School Outreach & Partnerships

Continuing motivational visits to schools like Mapetla High School, in partnership with the Skeem GP Foundation, touching thousands of learners' lives.

🏛️

Official Government Endorsement

Ward 16, City of Johannesburg Metropolitan Municipality
🤝

Partnership

Proudly partnering with the Skeem GP Foundation to deliver motivational and wellness programmes to high school learners across Soweto.
